# Epicast rules

- **Mandatory artwork placement and painting fidelity.** Every edit, repaint and animation of an approved Epicast asset uses the approved registered pose as its placement reference and the approved painting for its art direction. Lock the canvas, scale, orientation, anchors, silhouette and motif positions. Specify the editable region and any authorized motion in pixel coordinates before generation. Verify registration and painting fidelity before review, rendering or integration. Use registered masks for lighting-only edits and preserve original paint outside them. See the [registration and paint checks](docs/segmented-crest-artwork.md#lock-placement-and-painting-fidelity).

- **Standard segment texture bake, approved 2026-11-19.** Use size-specific premultiplied-alpha Lanczos2 followed by the shared linear-light, alpha-protected CAS bake at sharpness 1.0 and blend 0.65 for all shipped Epicast artwork segments and animation frames. Bake each size directly from its unsharpened registered source. Use Restoration as the reference for transparent TGA atlases, physical-pixel size selection and phase-preserving size changes. See the [runtime segment texture standard](docs/segmented-crest-artwork.md#bake-size-specific-runtime-textures).

- **Segmented crest artwork standard, approved 2026-11-18.** For every new layered crest or authorized segmentation of an existing design, follow the [segmented crest build guide](docs/segmented-crest-artwork.md). Generate complete movable pieces in isolation, register them with uniform scale and real underlap, and link contact shadows to their casters through lower receivers’ alpha masks. Preserve the editable master when baking loose assets. Restoration is the construction reference. Use the subject crest’s own approved motifs and painting direction.
