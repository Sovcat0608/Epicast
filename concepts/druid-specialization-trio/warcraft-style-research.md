# Warcraft style research for the Druid repaint

Researched 2026-11-16 for review v7. This is a practical synthesis for Epicast's small UI crests, not an official numbered Blizzard style guide.

## Primary sources

1. [World of Warcraft Student Art Contest Roundtable](https://magazine.artstation.com/2017/12/world-warcraft-student-art-contest-roundtable/), ArtStation Magazine, 2017. Direct discussion with Blizzard's WoW art team, including senior art director Chris Robinson. Robinson identifies hand-painted texturing and low-poly modeling as central to WoW's look. Art manager Jeff Parrott emphasizes whether a viewer can immediately identify what an object represents. For this 2D asset, the applicable principles are deliberate painted form and immediate recognition; low polygon counts are not a bitmap requirement.
2. [Artist Alex Horley Reflects on 15 Years of Working with Blizzard](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23591148/artist-alex-horley-reflects-on-15-years-of-working-with-blizzard), Blizzard interview by Dylan Walker. Horley explains resolving composition and values in small sketches, balancing light and shadow, and using colour relationships to retain brightness. This describes a Warcraft/Hearthstone illustrator's process, not a universal WoW engine rule. It supports judging these crests at small sizes and preserving value structure while increasing colour.
3. [Blizzard Insider #39 — Art of the Firelands](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/2993675/blizzard-insider-39-art-of-the-firelands), Blizzard interview with Jonathan Dumont and Gary Platner. They describe reducing visually confusing floating islands and lava waterfalls, and rejecting details that felt inconsistent with the intended environment. The relevant lesson is controlled complexity and thematic coherence, rather than copying Firelands materials.

## Application to this repaint

- **Readable shape and hierarchy:** retain each complete crest silhouette and the sprout / moon / paw focal motifs. Group highlights and shadows so these survive a small display size.
- **Hand-painted material character:** broad sculpted shading, selected bevels and long wood grooves; substantial ivory and stone planes; grouped fur locks and feather barbs.
- **Selective detail:** preserve the meaningful bracelets, leaf/feather patterns, medallions, charms and directional claw gouges. Remove dense crackle, speckles and uniform fine surface marks around them.
- **Controlled vibrance:** follow the user's supplied repaint for fresh green, violet, warm wood, cream and blue relationships. Dark recesses maintain contrast. This palette choice comes from the user's reference, not a claimed official palette rule.
- **Cohesive identity:** maintain shared Druid materials and matched visual weight, while each specialization keeps its own crown, central material, lower taper and thematic accents.

The smoothness target also follows the project's existing artwork-fidelity standard. The result is an image-generation repaint, not a blur/sharpen/saturation filter. Sources were researched as text; no claim is made to have watched an art-direction talk. Runtime sampling, motion and in-game acceptance are separate from this source-art review.
